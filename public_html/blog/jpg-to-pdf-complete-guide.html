What Is a JPG File?

JPG (or JPEG) is one of the most widely used image formats in the world. It is popular because it:

  • Compresses images to smaller sizes
  • Maintains decent image quality
  • Is supported by almost all devices

However, JPG files are not ideal for document sharing, especially when multiple pages or official formatting is required.

What Is a PDF File?

PDF (Portable Document Format) is designed to present documents consistently across all devices and operating systems. A PDF file:

  • Preserves layout and design
  • Is easy to print
  • Looks professional
  • Can contain multiple pages in one file

That's why converting JPG images to PDF makes documents easier to manage and share.
