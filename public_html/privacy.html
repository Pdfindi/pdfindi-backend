Privacy Policy

Your privacy and data security are our top priorities at PDFIndi

Our Commitment

At PDFIndi, we respect your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your data when you use our free online PDF tools. Our platform is built with a "Privacy-First" approach, supporting the Digital India vision for a secure digital ecosystem.

The PDFIndi Promise: We do not store your files permanently. All processed files are automatically deleted from our servers shortly after processing. We do not Sell or Share your personal data.

Information We Collect

1. File Data

When you upload a file for processing:

  • Temporary Storage: Files are stored temporarily on our secure servers only for the duration of processing.
  • Automatic Deletion: Files are automatically purged from our system within minutes (maximum 1 hour) after processing or conversion is complete.
  • No Human Access: No one at PDFIndi ever views the contents of your files. All processing is 100% automated.

2. Usage Data & Cookies

We collect anonymous data to improve our services:

  • Cookies: We use cookies to remember your preferences and ensure website functionality.
  • Analytics: We use Google Analytics to monitor traffic patterns and tool performance. This data is anonymized.
  • Advertising: We use Google AdSense to display ads, which helps keep our tools free. Google may use cookies to serve personalized ads.

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information:

  • ✅ SSL/TLS Encryption: All data transferred between your browser and our servers is encrypted.
  • ✅ Firewall Protection: Our servers are protected by advanced firewalls and security protocols.
  • ✅ Local Processing: Many of our tools perform processing directly in your browser, meaning your files never even leave your computer.
  • ✅ Indian Infrastructure: We prioritize hosted infrastructure in India to align with local data security standards.

Third-Party Services

PDFIndi integrates with trusted third-party services to provide high-quality features:

  • Google AdSense: Serves advertisements. Users can opt-out of personalized ads via Google Ad Settings.
  • Google Tag Manager & Analytics: Used for site performance tracking and optimization.
  • Cloudmersive/Adobe APIs: Some advanced conversion features may use secure, enterprise-grade APIs where data is also temporary and encrypted.

Your Rights & Control

You have full control over your privacy:

  • You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.
  • You are not required to register or provide personal information to use our tools.
  • You can contact us to request information about our data practices.
